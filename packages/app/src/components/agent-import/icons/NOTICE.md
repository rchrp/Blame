# Tool logos

Shown on the "Bring your setup" screen to tell the tools apart. The logos are
trademarks of their owners and are used only to name the tool.

The coloured ones (`claude-desktop`, `claude-code`, `codex`, `gemini`, `qwen`) are
drawn as they are; the others are single-colour and are drawn white on the
tool's colour. VS Code and Continue have no logo here and show their initials.
