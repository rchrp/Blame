## Is Blame really free?

Yes. The whole desktop app is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0: projects, the backlog, conversations, agents, local worktrees, and local previews, with your own AI providers. [Blame Premium](/docs/account/free-and-premium) only pays for services that Blame hosts.

## Do I need an account?

No. The first screen offers to sign in or to continue without an account. Without an account, everything stays on your computer; a free account adds sync between computers, web research for agents, deployment to Vercel, and the Premium services. See [Using Blame without an account](/docs/account/without-an-account).

## Where does my code run?

On your computer. Local execution uses a Lima VM on macOS, a WSL distribution on Windows, and native worktrees with Docker on Linux. Code is sent to the AI provider you choose when an agent needs it. Cloud Previews, a Premium feature, sync a workspace snapshot to a managed sandbox. See [Isolation](/docs/concepts/isolation).

## Which AI providers can I use?

Any of the 50 supported providers, with your own API key or subscription, plus any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. You can sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT plan, and use a different model for each agent. See [AI providers](/docs/guides/ai-providers).

## Who pays for the AI models?

Your AI provider bills its own usage, with or without Premium.

## What can Blame preview?

Web apps and APIs that run on Linux, in a built-in browser, and Linux GUI, Electron, and Tauri apps, as a screen of the running app. Previews for CLI tools, iOS and Android apps, and native macOS and Windows apps are in development. See [Previews](/docs/guides/previews).

## Does Blame send telemetry?

Not without an account. With an account, the app sends usage metrics, never prompts, code, or keys, and you can turn them off. See [Privacy and telemetry](/docs/account/privacy-and-telemetry).

## Can I self-host the backend?

For one person, it already is: without an account, the desktop app runs the same API on your computer, on SQLite. Hosting it for a team is not turnkey yet. The development setup in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/rchrp/Blame/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) runs the whole stack locally.

## Can I use Blame commercially?

Yes. If you distribute Blame, you must provide the corresponding source under the same license. If you modify Blame and let users interact with it over a network, you must offer them the source of your modified version, as section 13 of the [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/rchrp/Blame/blob/main/LICENSE) requires.

## How do I report a bug?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/rchrp/Blame/issues/new/choose) with the steps to reproduce it, your platform and Blame version, and the relevant logs. For questions, use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/rchrp/Blame/discussions) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/AfjG6aUyxe).
