Blame needs macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. The app is signed and notarized.

## Blame Runtime

On first launch, Blame sets up a small Linux VM, based on [Lima](https://lima-vm.io), where agents, worktrees, and previews run. Blame calls it **Blame Runtime**, and manages it in **Settings → Desktop → Blame Runtime**:

- **Install**, **Repair**, or **Check again**, depending on its state;
- **Start over → Reset** deletes the sandbox and installs a fresh one. Your projects are not touched; running local previews stop.

If Blame Runtime can't start, install, or update, Blame first repairs it by itself. If that fails, it shows **Blame Runtime needs a repair**: **Repair** reinstalls the parts that are broken, and your projects are not touched. **Start over** resets it.

While the runtime is not ready, you can still work: choose **Use Direct instead** to run a story in your project folder, without isolation, or use a [Cloud Preview](/docs/guides/previews#where-a-story-runs).

### "Local containers aren't available on this computer"

Blame Runtime needs macOS 13 or later. On an older version, run stories in the cloud or directly in your project.

## Logs

- The app's log: `~/Library/Application Support/dev.blame.desktop/logs/blame-live.log`
- Each story's setup, agent, and merge logs: `.blame/logs/` in its worktree
- A story's preview: its **Run logs** tab
