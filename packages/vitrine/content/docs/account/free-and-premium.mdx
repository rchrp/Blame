Blame is free and open source (AGPL-3.0). Blame Premium pays for hosted services that cost money to run.

| Free, no account needed              | Blame Premium (with an account)                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Projects, backlog, and conversations | Cloud voice transcription                                     |
| Local worktrees and local previews   | Managed Cloud Previews                                        |
| Your own AI provider credentials     | New hosted file uploads, including deployment source archives |

A free account adds sync between computers, web research for agents, and deployment to Vercel.

## Price

Premium is **$20/month** or **$200/year**. Existing usage limits apply. See [blame.so/pricing](/pricing).

## Who pays for the AI models

Agents use your own API key or subscription with the provider you choose, and that provider bills its own usage, with or without Premium.

## When a subscription ends

Your files stay accessible. Ending a subscription stops the hosted services, such as new hosted uploads and Cloud Previews, not your access to the files you already uploaded.

## Cancel or get a refund

You can cancel anytime from the billing portal, and paid access lasts until the end of the current period. Eligible consumers can withdraw within 14 days of subscribing, and a valid withdrawal is refunded in full. See the [refund policy](/refund-policy).
