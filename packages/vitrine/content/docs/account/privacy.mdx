## No telemetry

Blame sends no telemetry: no usage metrics, traces, logs, or crash reports, with or without an account. Agents call only the AI provider you pick.

## Update check

At launch, the app asks `download.blame.so` whether a newer version exists. To turn it off, open **Settings → Desktop → Privacy** and switch off **Check for updates at launch**.

On Windows, the installer offers the same switch before installing.

## With an account

Signing in syncs your workspace with `api.blame.so` and adds web research and the Blame Cloud services (see [Using Blame without an account](/docs/account/without-an-account)). Blame's servers keep technical records of their own requests, such as the route, status, and duration, to stay reliable.

## Reporting a problem

When a runtime setup or repair fails, or the app crashes, Blame offers **Report on GitHub**. It opens a new issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/rchrp/Blame/issues) in your browser, filled in with the app version, your system and architecture, and the error shown. Paths in your home folder appear as `~`.

You review and edit the issue, and it is posted only if you submit it. The app sends nothing.

## Where your code goes

- Agents, worktrees, and previews run on your computer (see [Isolation](/docs/concepts/isolation)).
- Code is sent to the AI provider you choose when an agent needs it.
- Cloud Previews, part of Blame Cloud, sync a workspace snapshot to a managed sandbox.
