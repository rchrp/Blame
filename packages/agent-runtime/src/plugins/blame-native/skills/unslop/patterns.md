## Concrete corrections

- **Headings.** Remove “OUR SERVICES” above “Services”. Give sibling cards short titles one level below the page heading. “Welcome back!” on a financial summary can give way to the actual reporting period.
- **Numbers.** Permute three service cards marked 01, 02, 03. If their meaning survives, remove the numbers. Keep step numbers in a checkout where the sequence matters.
- **Type.** Set “Billing address” in the interface face. Reserve terminal-style monospace for content whose characters matter, such as a commit hash. A serif headline can fit a literary journal. Repeating it across settings panels just to suggest luxury weakens their hierarchy.
- **Case and icons.** “🔥  PAID THIS MONTH” becomes “Paid this month”. Prefer sentence case to rows of spaced capitals in headings and labels. Keep meaningful acronyms. Remove decorative emoji, including the redundant icon that might replace one. Give icon-only actions a consistent glyph and accessible name.
- **Color.** A glowing search field, violet sidebar and four bright totals compete. Quiet the surrounding surfaces. Spend the brand accent on the next action or a meaningful selection. Pair status color with text. In a chart, give each series a consistent color and label.
- **Composition and copy.** Replace three promo cards above a departures list with the next departure and a booking action. Keep a real comparison period beside a percentage. Remove invented metrics, logo strips and placeholder claims.
- **Spacing.** If a group label sits midway between two lists, move it closer to the list it introduces. Align the first letters of menu items even when the icons differ in width.

For a stubborn redesign, consult `references/signs.md`, or `references/tokens.md` for spacing and type. Adapt those defaults to the project's brand and accessible contrast. If seeing a worked comparison would settle a design choice, call `skills` with `skill="unslop", args="example"`.
