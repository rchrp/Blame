Light still exposes five primitives: search/read navigate declarations and observed source; edit plans guarded symbol/patch changes; run owns command/process results; finish evaluates delivery and available validation evidence. Compiler identity/rename applies to eligible configured JS/TS projects. Lexical fallback, execution evidence, admitted verification and user criteria remain distinct. Research adapters below do not silently enter production.
