Decision: **retain** the bounded validation-history/admission correction, passive time observer and stronger finite controls for their tested accuracy and diagnostic value. **Reject** an efficiency claim at equal overall Cline quality or a model-benefit claim for the unexposed receipt. **Investigate next**, as separately registered experiments: (1) an identical post-validation source/test edit handoff to each arm, including optional-compiler claims; (2) clear reporting of ignored wrapper arguments and real delivered-test compilation; (3) lower tool/HTTP round-trip overhead on preselected larger multi-file tasks. Keep these diagnostics separate from fresh-task confirmation. Preserve executed tests, passed tests, admitted verification and user acceptance as different claims.
